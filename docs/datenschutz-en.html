2. Data processed by the app

Installation identifier: On first use, the app creates a random UUID (installation_id) and stores it locally in app preferences. This is not generated from your name or Apple advertising ID. It does, however, allow the app installation to be recognized again, so related events are not fully anonymous.

Favorites: Favorites are saved locally. When you add or remove one, the action, wallpaper ID and installation identifier are sent to a Supabase endpoint. The server stores the association and its timestamp. Removing a favorite deletes the corresponding server favorite record.

Downloads: After a wallpaper is saved, the app sends a download event containing the wallpaper ID, installation identifier and an event ID. These events contribute to overall popularity rankings. Events that could not be delivered are kept temporarily in a local queue and retried later.

These events support favorites and general popularity rankings. They are not linked to an advertising identifier. Where no more specific legal basis applies, processing for these purposes relies on our legitimate interests in providing and improving the app (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). You may object on grounds relating to your particular situation under Article 21 GDPR. Where consent is legally required for a processing activity, that consent must be obtained before collection.

3. Catalog, images, Photos and sharing

The app obtains catalog information from Supabase. For images, it requests time-limited signed Cloudflare R2 URLs where needed, and may cache image files locally. Network requests necessarily involve connection data such as IP address, time, requested resources and device/browser metadata. These requests are made to provide the content you select (GDPR Article 6(1)(b), where a user agreement applies, or Article 6(1)(f) otherwise).

iOS may request permission to add wallpaper files to Photos. Sharing uses the native iOS share sheet; subsequent processing by a selected third-party app follows that provider's policies. Previously downloaded images may remain in the app cache.

4. Service providers and international transfers

Supabase provides catalog and event storage, database and app endpoints, with the project configured for eu-west-1. Cloudflare R2 delivers image files, with storage configured for Western Europe. GitHub Pages hosts this website, and Microsoft Outlook.com handles email inquiries. These providers may process technical connection data. Regional storage settings do not rule out all transfers outside the EEA. Where international transfers occur, the applicable provider terms, safeguards and data protection requirements apply; details are available in the providers' privacy and contractual information.

Read more: Supabase, Cloudflare, GitHub, Microsoft.

5. Retention and deletion

The local installation identifier and favorites currently have no fixed automatic expiry in app data. Removing the app may cause iOS to remove local app data. Undelivered events remain locally until successfully submitted. Removing a favorite deletes its matching server favorite record. Stored download events currently have no automatic time-based deletion; a 30-day analytics window is not a deletion period. Technical logs and backups are subject to the relevant providers' retention processes. To request removal of installation-related events, contact us at the email above; handling and any necessary linkage to your installation are assessed case by case. Uninstalling the app does not automatically erase server records.

6. Website and support

As currently implemented, this static website does not embed its own analytics scripts, tracking cookies or externally hosted fonts. GitHub Pages processes connection information needed to serve pages. If you send a support or privacy request, we process the sender address, message and any attachments to respond (GDPR Article 6(1)(b) or (f), depending on the request; Article 6(1)(c) where legal obligations apply). Messages are retained only as needed for responding, substantiating a matter or meeting legal obligations.

7. Your rights

Subject to the GDPR, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing. You may withdraw consent, where relevant, with effect for the future. You can lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority, including the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA). Contact the email address above to exercise your rights.

8. Updates

This policy may be updated as app features and processing practices change. The version published here applies.
