Dinamic Wallpaper lets users browse wallpapers, mark favorites, and save or share images using iOS features. An internet connection is required to fetch new content. Previously loaded files may be cached, but complete offline availability is not promised.

Origin of wallpapers

According to the operator, the currently available wallpapers were created with AI assistance in ChatGPT. Other image providers are planned but not yet used. The provenance and applicable rights of any later additions must be confirmed before publication.

Personal use as wallpapers

The intended license allows users to store images provided through Dinamic Wallpaper on their personal devices and use them only as non-commercial personal wallpapers. Necessary cropping and resizing are included. Where the operator holds relevant rights, the permission is non-exclusive.

Uses not covered

Commercial exploitation is not included. In particular, users may not resell images, offer them as paid downloads or part of products for sale, or republish them as part of another wallpaper collection, wallpaper app or download service, even if that collection is free. Additional uses require an individual agreement to the extent that the operator is entitled to grant such rights. Use of the app's share feature does not expand these permissions. Mandatory statutory rights remain unaffected.

AI-generated content

No blanket claim of exclusive copyright protection is made for all AI-generated images. The proposed terms describe the intended contractual scope of use; they do not create rights against all third parties or automatically apply to independently created similar images. Applicable rights depend on individual circumstances.

Before release: Document image creation and relevant source material or provider terms, review third-party rights for each image and decide how final terms are incorporated into the app.

Before release: Confirm coverage, formation of terms, intended audience/minimum age, app usage rights, availability, acceptable use and termination. Any liability provisions must respect mandatory consumer rights.
